The Pub

Proper pub. Proper beer.

Six handpumps of ever-changing cask ales from Hattie Brown's, Exeter, St Austell, Cheddar Ales, Piddle and more, alongside traditional ciders and some of the best-value pints in town.

Pump clips from the beers we've poured cover the beams. Grab a stool by the fire, catch the big match, or head out to one of our two courtyard gardens.

Our story

A family pub on Barrack Street

The Tiger has stood on Barrack Street since the 18th century and is protected as a grade II listed building.

Today it's owned and run by Graham and Jacquie Taylor and their family as a true freehouse: cosy, traditional and proud to champion West Country brewers. Pop in and you'll probably meet one of the family behind the bar.
